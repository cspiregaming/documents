# Officer Interest Period — 2026

Schedule and announcement draft for this year's annual Officer selection process,
per [bylaws.md](bylaws.md) Article IV (Annual Interest Survey Period) and
Article V (Voting Process).

## Timeline

| Phase | Date | Bylaws basis |
|---|---|---|
| Notify members | On or before Mon, Oct 19, 2026 | Art. IV.2 — Administrators notify at/before commencement |
| Interest survey open | Mon, Oct 19 – Sat, Oct 31, 2026 | Art. IV.1 — third Monday of October through end of month |
| Officer confirmation vote | Mon, Nov 16, 2026 | Art. V.1 — third Monday of November, special Officer meeting |
| Nominees notified (C Spire email) | Promptly after Nov 16 vote | Art. IV.3 |
| Accept/decline deadline | 3 days after nominee notification | Art. IV.3 |
| Roster/site update | By ~Nov 23, 2026 (after acceptances in) | — |

## Discord announcement draft

```
🌟 **Officer Interest Survey — Now Open!**

Every year, C Spire Gaming opens a window for members to step into an Officer
role. If you've been active in the community and want a bigger hand in
running events, moderating, or shaping what we do next — this is your window.

📋 **Eligibility** (per club bylaws, Art. III)
• Active member — you've participated in 50%+ of club activities in the
  last 6 months
• Current C Spire employee
• Not currently under disciplinary action

📝 **How to apply**
Submit your interest to Micah Smith by email or via https://dyno.gg/form/1cf4f522
before the survey closes on **Saturday, Oct 31**.

🗳️ **What happens next**
Current Officers vote on candidates at a special meeting on **Monday,
Nov 16**. If selected, you'll be notified at your C Spire email and will
have 3 days to accept.

❓ **Questions?**
Tag any officer in #general-gaming or DM us.

Thanks for being part of the community! 🎮
```
